08FAQ

Questions before a first conversation.

The things businesses usually ask before getting in touch.

What is Senior Finance Support?
Senior Finance Support is a dedicated finance resource for your business — providing structure, oversight, and strategic input without the cost or commitment of a full-time hire. Think of it as having a Finance Director in your corner, scaled to where your business actually is.
How are you different from a traditional accountant?
Traditional accounting is important — but by nature it's backward-looking. We take a forward-looking approach, translating your numbers into strategic insights that drive better decisions. We're invested in your success, not just your compliance.
What size businesses do you work with?
We work with small businesses, SMEs, and startups — typically with annual revenues between £50k and £5m. Whether you're a solo founder or an established business with a growing team, we tailor our services to your size, complexity, and ambitions.
Do I need a bookkeeper or accountant already in place?
We highly recommend it. Clean, consistent data is the foundation everything else is built on. If you don't have that in place yet, we'll tell you upfront and help you understand what needs to be sorted before we can add the most value.
What accounting software do you work with?
We work with all major cloud accounting platforms including Xero, QuickBooks, and Microsoft 365. If you're not yet using cloud software, we can help you choose the right platform and manage the migration.
How quickly can you get started?
We can typically onboard a new client within one to two weeks of the initial discovery call. The timeline depends on the state of your current records — if the books need tidying first, we'll tell you upfront, with no surprises.
What does pricing look like?
Our pricing is transparent and based on the scope of services, business complexity, and transaction volume. We offer fixed monthly packages — no surprise invoices, no hidden fees. A tailored quote follows your discovery call.
Do you offer one-off projects or only ongoing services?
Both. While our core offering is ongoing monthly support, we also take on one-off engagements: financial health assessments, software setup, historical bookkeeping cleanup, and process improvements — a great way to experience the Cuno approach before committing.
